# Privacy Policy for Apuntador

**Last updated: October 12, 2025**

## Overview

Apuntador ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Apuntador mobile application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

## Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**

Apuntador is designed to work entirely offline and locally on your device. All text processing, file handling, and app functionality occurs exclusively on your device.

## Data Storage

- **Local Storage Only**: All documents, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device
- **No Cloud Sync**: We do not sync your data to any external servers
- **No User Accounts**: The app does not require registration or user accounts

## Data We DO NOT Collect

- Personal identification information
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Usage analytics
- Crash reports
- Network activity
- File contents or documents

## Third-Party Services

Apuntador does not integrate with any third-party services that collect data.

## Device Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:
- **Storage Access**: To save and load your teleprompter scripts locally
- **Screen Orientation**: To rotate the display as needed

These permissions are used solely for app functionality and no data is transmitted externally.

## Data Security

Since no data leaves your device, your privacy is protected by your device's own security measures. We recommend:
- Keeping your device updated
- Using device lock screens
- Regular backups using your device's built-in backup features

## Children's Privacy

Our Service is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
